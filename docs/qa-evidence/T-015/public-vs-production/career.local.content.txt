TEXT Company Description:
TEXT Fashion Asia Ltd. is a leading knit garments manufacturing company Sister Concern of Northern Tosrifa Group, located in Sreepur, Gazipur, Bangladesh. The company specializes in manufacturing high-quality knit apparel for international buyers and brands.
TEXT Fashion Asia Ltd. is committed to maintaining high standards of quality, compliance, efficiency, and on-time delivery throughout its manufacturing operations. The company has dedicated teams across Merchandising, Quality, Production, Industrial Engineering, Planning & Coordination, Commercial, Marketing, HR & Administration, and Supply Chain, working together to ensure smooth and efficient operations.
